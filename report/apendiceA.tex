\chapter{Declaration on the use of generative AI}
\label{app:ai}

This appendix declares every use of generative AI in this project, both as a component of the laboratory and as a tool in producing the software and this report. The use of AI tools in the report is permitted by the rules of the programme, provided that it is declared.

\section{Generative AI as a component of the laboratory}
\label{sec:ai-component}

The laboratory calls the Gemini API~\cite{geminiapi} on three routes: phishing generation (\path{/api/generate-phishing}), email analysis (\path{/api/analyze-email}) and the narration of non-preset deepfake scenarios (\path{/api/analyze-media-deepfake}). Every response from these routes carries a \texttt{source} field that states whether Gemini, a deterministic fallback or a stored preset produced it. The default model identifier is \texttt{gemini-3.5-flash}, configurable through the environment variable \texttt{GEMINI\_MODEL}; the recorded calls in \path{eval/gemini_runs/} were served by that identifier. Gemini is not used on the adversarial route and does not produce any number reported in Chapter~\ref{cha:eval}.

\section{Responsibility of the author}
\label{sec:ai-author}

The author defined the problem, the scope, the requirements and the design decisions described in this report, including the provenance contract, the exclusion of media processing and mail sending, the choice of the CIFAR-10 model and the security controls. The author ran the evaluation scripts that produced the result files, checked every number in Chapter~\ref{cha:eval} against those files, and reviewed and corrected all AI-drafted text. AI tools did not produce any result reported in this document: all results come from the scripts in the repository and can be regenerated as described in Appendix~\ref{app:eval}.
